# Paper-cut card — template

One small, shippable improvement. Copy this into a sprint PLAN.md or a
product BACKLOG.md. Anything bigger than M gets split — no exceptions.

### PC-N: <Title> (S|M)

- **Problem:** <one line — the pain, not the solution>
- **In scope:** <exactly what will change>
- **Out of scope:** <what will NOT change, spelled out — this is the
  scope-creep shield>
- **Done looks like:** <how the founder will test it, on a real device>
- **Known risk:** <optional — the honest worry, and the design answer>
- **Constitution check:** <which VISION.md non-goals were checked —
  "passes" or the specific conflict>

Sizes: **S** < 1 hour · **M** ≈ half a day.
